SCHOOL & CLASSROOM INFORMATION

Policies & Resources

Information about grading, late assignments, and opportunities for students to revise, redo, and demonstrate their learning.

01

Grading Policy

Berea Middle School, operating under Greenville County Schools rules, follows an academic grading system designed to build responsibility while supporting content mastery.

Grading System & Scale

  • A: 90–100
  • B: 80–89
  • C: 70–79
  • D: 60–69
  • F: 0–59

Grades reflect the numerical score earned by the student for their performance.

Weight Distribution

  • 50% Major Assessments: Tests, formal lab reports, comprehensive writing assignments, and major projects.
  • 50% Minor Assessments: Quizzes, daily classwork, warm-ups, and minor class assignments.

02

Late Work Policy

Students are expected to turn in all assignments. When a deadline is missed, the assignment may be recorded as Not Handed In (NHI) or Zero in the grade book.

Late Assignment Window

Unexcused late work may be accepted within the designated school or team deadline, generally within 5 to 7 school days of the original due date, or until the final grading period ends, whichever comes first.

Penalties

Depending on the class structure or team expectations, assignments submitted within the designated grace period may be accepted without a point reduction. Work submitted after the applicable deadline may receive a permanent zero.

Recovery & Support

Students are encouraged to address missing assignments through designated OnTrack Time, flex-time remediation, or available pre-school or after-school tutoring.

03

Redo / Revise / Retake

Because the goal of instruction is mastery, students may have opportunities to reassess their learning and demonstrate improved understanding.

Major Assessments

Retakes or redos apply to major tests and projects. Retake opportunities are not provided for minor daily assignments or quizzes.

One Opportunity

Students are allowed one retake opportunity per major assessment.

Prerequisites & Deadlines

Students should initiate a retake request within 5 school days of receiving the original grade. Teachers may require relearning activities, such as test corrections, tutoring, or an additional study plan, before a reassessment.

Late Work

A major assessment that was originally submitted late is not eligible for a redo or retake.
