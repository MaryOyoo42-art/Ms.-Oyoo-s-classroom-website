GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

My goal is to create an environment where every student feels valued, supported, respected, and encouraged to reach their full potential.

I am a 7th grade inclusion teacher and case manager with 10 years of teaching experience, including three years at Berea Middle School.

I spent seven years in Kenya teaching Kenyan Sign Language, Mathematics, and Physics to students who were deaf or hard of hearing. My experience across Kenya and the United States has strengthened my commitment to inclusive education, differentiated instruction, and meaningful collaboration.

I enjoy helping students grow academically and personally while building on their unique strengths. My goal is to ensure every student feels valued, supported, and encouraged to reach their full potential.

Education & Certifications

  • Bachelor of Education in Special Education
